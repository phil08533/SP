# Characters, Factions, and Dialogue

## Dialogue rules
Characters speak with specificity, not generic quest-giver language. Keep lines short during play. Humor reveals personality or local logic; avoid constant quips, exposition dumps, and slang that dates quickly. NPCs may lie or be mistaken, but critical mission information must be recoverable from another cue. Dialogue is specified as speaker, trigger, line, optional choice, and state effect.

## Core cast
### Mara Venn — mechanic / reluctant mentor
Broad work jacket, raised welding visor, grease-dark gloves, asymmetrical haircut, oversized key ring. Blunt, competent, kind through complaints. Wants to keep the garage solvent and the town independent. She repaired a Pumpworks service cart before the incident and knows the official account is incomplete. Offers vehicle repairs, practical jobs, and evidence leads.
- First meeting: “If it rattles, it's either loose or trying to tell you something. Around here, check both.”
- Portal: “I fix machines. I don't fix whatever that thing thinks a machine is.”
- After a successful return: “You came back with all your limbs and most of your pockets. That's a better start than most.”
- Ask for work: “There's a courier fee if you don't ask what's in the box. There's a higher fee if you do.”
- Repeated trouble: “I can repair a bumper. I can't repair a reputation. That's your department.”

### Finch Rill — eccentric / pattern spotter
Oversized rain poncho in dry weather, homemade antenna pack, three mismatched lenses. Funny and observant; occasionally right for the wrong reasons. Treat him as perceptive, not a caricature. Gives rumors, route hints, rare-specimen clues.
- “The drain isn't haunted. Haunted things have manners. That drain is curious.”
- “Count the green flashes. Don't count the blue ones; they count you back.”
- “I don't need you to believe me. I need you to stand over there and tell me if the shadow has elbows.”
- “The flowers aren't pointing at the sun. They're pointing at the bit of sky that forgot to be there.”

### Jo Sable — Lucky Comet diner owner
Patterned apron, chrome forearm brace, sleepy eyes, immaculate counter. Warm but discerning; keeps the diner open and local kids out of dangerous work. Provides legal jobs, gossip, and local context.
- “Coffee's hot. News is stale. Both'll keep you awake.”
- “If somebody says a deal is risk-free, ask why they're paying you.”
- “Want to help? Take this crate to Mara. Want to get rich? First learn which roads flood when the sky is clear.”

### Officer Imani Vale — local law enforcement
Practical patrol coat, worn boots, cracked body-camera casing, reflective stripe. Measured, observant, dryly funny; not corrupt by default or omniscient. Provides warnings, fines, legal work, and optional informant path.
- First warning: “I'm not arresting you for looking strange. This town would be empty by lunch.”
- Investigating cargo: “Hands where I can see them. Then tell me why the municipal gate is open and you're carrying a glowing plant.”
- If cooperative: “That was the easiest conversation I've had all week. Please don't make me regret it.”
- Informant offer: “I don't need a hero. I need somebody who notices things and knows when to keep their mouth shut.”
- If evaded: “You can run. The paperwork is faster than you think.”

### Rook Calder — salvage broker / gray-market buyer
Patched long coat, polished boots, clicking prosthetic finger, desk made from a vehicle hood. Charming and transactional; never tells the whole deal unless useful. Wants to control anomalous-material trade before an outside corporation arrives. Offers risky buyers and deliveries.
- “Raw material is a promise. Processed material is a promise somebody already paid to break.”
- “I don't ask where it came from. I do ask whether it hums when the lights are off.”
- “You'll get a fair price. Fair for me, fair for you, and unfair to whoever thought of it first.”
- Refuse a deal: “Excellent. Keep your principles. Bring them back when they need gas money.”

### Dr. Sera Quill — former Pumpworks researcher
Faded lab jacket, sample jars used as pen cups, diagrams on receipts. Precise about evidence, evasive about personal responsibility. Identifies samples and unlocks safer collection/research later.
- “It isn't a portal in the spiritual sense. It's a boundary condition with terrible manners.”
- “The plant isn't glowing. It's moving energy somewhere else. The glow is what leaks.”
- “I filed a report. The report was filed under ‘equipment noise.’ The equipment has since developed a horizon.”

## Supporting cast
- **Tamsin Pike:** motel manager; trades local rumors for small favors.
- **Bex and Boro:** sibling salvage collectors who disagree about value but always work together.
- **“Weather” Wes:** radio host who describes impossible events as local weather.
- **Nell-7:** battered municipal maintenance drone with outdated rules and unexpectedly strong opinions.
- **The Quiet Customer:** appears at the diner at irregular times; never confirms whether they are human.

## Factions
- **Town Mutuals:** residents preserving local services; trust grows through repairs, deliveries, and low-harm problem solving.
- **Pumpworks Authority:** thinly staffed municipal department protecting the portal site and concealing embarrassing failures; not a monolithic evil empire.
- **Calder's Exchange:** loose network of brokers and runners; useful but untrustworthy.
- **Lumen Survey:** off-site research organization initially represented through notes, equipment, and Sera. Do not build a large corporate faction in the first slice.

Keep faction state as a few reputation bands and flags, not a giant branching morality tree.

## Opening mission: “A Small Favor”
Trigger: approach Mara's Garage after leaving the hideout.

Mara: “You looking for work, or did that car stop on its own?”
Choices:
1. “Work, if it pays.” → “Good. There's a sealed case needs moving to the old Pumpworks gate. Don't open it. Don't leave it in the sun. Don't ask why it ticks.”
2. “What's wrong with the town?” → “Depends who you ask. The council says budget. The diner says weather. I say somebody built a door where a wall used to be.”
3. “I can fix my own car.” → “Then you won't mind if I charge you for the tools you break.”

At Pumpworks, the case indicator pulses in sync with the portal. Finch calls from the fence: “That's the wrong kind of ticking. That one is counting down to a place.”

The player can deliver the case for a small legal reward or investigate the portal and discover the resource route. Leaving early must not soft-lock the mission.

## Dialogue state rules
Use bark pools with cooldowns. Named dialogue responds to mission and relationship state. Subtitles are required. Do not gate mission completion on a single hidden line or one NPC being at a precise schedule location.
